# Shop owner handover

## What this site does

- **Get directions** opens the shop's [Google Maps pin](https://maps.app.goo.gl/141XQMZtooLVQQN29?g_st=ic) near Bhavani Sweet Home at Shivaji Chowk, Pimple Nilakh.
- **Call** opens a dialer with the confirmed salon number. The site also displays regular hours: Sunday and Tuesday–Saturday, 7:30 AM–10:00 PM; Monday closed.
- **Instagram** opens the [salon’s business profile](https://www.instagram.com/the_kohinoor_salon/). Customers can ask about a haircut, prices, or availability there. You reply and confirm a time yourself.
- The site does not take bookings, hold time slots, or collect customer details. It does not add a phone number, hours, prices, or photos without your confirmation.

## Before sharing a public link

Open the approved site on a phone. Tap Directions and check that it opens the correct shop pin. Tap Instagram and verify it opens the business profile. Tap Call to verify the dialer shows the shop number without placing a call. No test message needs to be sent. A file or a private preview isn’t the public website; use the address the maintainer confirms after launch.

## Making updates

Tell the maintainer the exact text to change. For opening hours, include the days and any exceptions. For a phone or WhatsApp link, confirm the business number and which contact option you want customers to use. For prices, give the exact amount, service, and any conditions. Only send photos you own or have permission to publish, including permission from anyone identifiable in them.

If a detail changes, tell the maintainer which page or button is affected. Don’t send passwords, sign-in codes, or customer information. The maintainer should verify changes in a preview before replacing the live site.
